## Standalone: accounts kept by Nid

- **The first administrator**: on first start, Nid writes a one-time address in its log (`docker compose logs nid | grep setup`) that creates their account.
- **The second adult**: the administrator adds them in Administration › Utilisateurs, then creates their access link from the row's menu. The link works once, for 72 hours; the adult picks their identifier and password there (at least 12 characters).
- **Two-step verification** is set by each adult on their Sécurité page, with an authenticator app and 10 recovery codes.

## Reference: Pocket ID

Pocket ID decides through groups: `nid-users` grants access, `nid-admins` the administration of Nid. Nid creates the adult on their first sign-in, within the household's limit (2 adults by default). The administrator role gives no right over the other adult's private items.

## Then, in Nid

- Fill in the household's people (Réglages › Personnes): birth dates, children.
- Add your accounts and investments, a loan, your subscriptions, or import a file.
- Each month, the routine updates the values in a few minutes.
