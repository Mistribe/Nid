The home page answers the household's questions in under a minute. Every figure comes from the server, with its date: a value nobody knows is shown as « unknown », never as zero.

## The questions it answers

- Where do we stand, in total and per person?
- What is left this month, once the commitments are paid?
- What changed since last month?
- What is coming in the next few weeks?

## What Nid does

- **Four key figures**: net worth, net income per month, commitments per month and what is left to live on.
- **Where the month's income goes**: a diagram that follows each income to subscriptions, loans, property charges and what remains.
- **The net worth curve** over 12 months, 3 years or since the start, with cumulative contributions next to the value.
- **A net worth projection** over 5, 10, 20 or 30 years, or to retirement, with a « 9 cases out of 10 » range.
- **Since last month**: what moved, and why (investment value, principal repaid, contributions).
- **Coming up and To do**: renewals, payments, contract ends, values to update, proposals to review, with a monthly calendar.
- **The monthly routine**: under ten minutes to update the values, last month's value offered on each line.
- **The yearly review**, printable: the household's year on one page.

## The terms

- **Net worth**: what the household owns (accounts, investments, property) minus what it owes (loan principal left).
- **Commitments**: the spending planned each month: subscriptions, loan payments, property charges, childcare.
- **What is left**: the month's income minus the commitments, before everyday spending.
- **Value to update**: an investment value older than its envelope's usual rhythm; Nid flags it rather than trust it.
