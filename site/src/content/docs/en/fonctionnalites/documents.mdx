Tax notices, deeds, amortization tables, yearly statements: Nid keeps the household's documents and links them to the item they concern. It also knows which ones are missing.

## The questions it answers

- Where is the flat's deed?
- Which documents are we missing for this year?
- Where is this loan's amortization table?

<Screen name="documents" alt="The demo household's documents: the drop zone and the list of expected documents." />

## What Nid does

- **Upload** PDFs, images or e-mails, up to 25 MB.
- **Each document is linked** to the property, loan, envelope or person it concerns.
- **Expected documents**: what is missing for each item, and a button to upload it.
- **« À classer »** (to file) for what is not linked yet, and filters by type, year and item.
- **An assistant** can read a statement, a payslip or an amortization table and propose the values, which you approve.

## The terms

- **To file**: uploaded documents linked to no item of the household yet.
