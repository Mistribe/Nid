Nid brings a household's finances together in one place. Each area below has its page: what it does, the questions it answers, what it looks like, and the terms it uses.

The screenshots show the demo household, Alice, Bob and their child Léa: invented people and figures. The interface is in French for now, English is coming soon. [Try the demo](https://demo.nid.mistribe.com) to browse these screens yourself.
