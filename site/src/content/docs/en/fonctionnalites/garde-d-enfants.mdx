Employing a childminder in France means a contract, a salary smoothed over the year, paid leave and a Pajemploi declaration every month. Nid prepares all of it from the contract, and tracks what the state pays back.

## The questions it answers

- How much do we declare to Pajemploi this month?
- How many days of paid leave has the childminder earned?
- What does childcare really cost, once the benefits are deducted?

## What Nid does

- **The contract** with the childminder, or with a home nanny.
- **Each month's statement**, ready to copy into Pajemploi.
- **Paid leave** earned and taken.
- **The CMG childcare benefit**, estimated.
- **The end of the contract** and its final settlement.
- **The childcare tax credit**, carried into the Tax module.

## The terms

- **Pajemploi**: the French Urssaf service where a childminder's or home nanny's salary is declared each month.
- **CMG**: « complément de libre choix du mode de garde », a CAF benefit that covers part of the cost.
- **Smoothed salary**: the salary spread over the year, paid the same each month whatever the weeks of care.
