Nid plugs into your AI assistant (Claude Desktop or any MCP-compatible assistant), and has one inside the app. The assistant reads your figures and answers your questions; it can propose a change, but nothing is saved until a person approves it.

## The questions it answers

- How much is left this month?
- Can you record this life insurance statement?
- Which subscription went up the most this year?

## What Nid does

- **An MCP server**: your assistant finds read tools there, and write tools that only propose.
- **« À valider »** (to review): each proposal shows what it would change next to the current state; you approve, edit or reject it.
- **The assistant inside the app**, with a local or a cloud model (a cloud provider is enough, no worker to install), and conversations kept for their author only.
- **Personal access tokens**, with their scope, revocable at any time.
- **The AI never computes**: a gain, a return, a schedule or a tax always comes from the server.

## The terms

- **MCP**: Model Context Protocol, the standard that lets an AI assistant use outside tools, here Nid's.
- **Proposal**: a change suggested by an assistant, a document or the bank, waiting in « À valider ».
- **Access token**: a personal key given to an assistant, limited to what you allow.
