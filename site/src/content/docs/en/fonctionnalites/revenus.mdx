Nid tracks each adult's income: salaries and their changes, bonuses, rents. It works out a monthly income smoothed over the year, the one behind what is left to live on and the debt ratio.

## The questions it answers

- How much does the household earn per month, bonuses included?
- How has our income changed over three years?
- Is our withholding rate the right one?

<Screen name="revenus" alt="The demo household's income overview: net income per month, the year's total and month by month." />

## What Nid does

- **Salaries and their phases**: a raise, part-time work, a contract end.
- **Payslips**: the expected net and gross compared with the payslip's.
- **One-off income**: a bonus, profit-sharing, a sale.
- **Rents** of the current leases, counted without charges.
- **The withholding rate** compared with the real rate the Tax module estimates.
- **Visibility**: each adult chooses whether their income is visible to the other.

## The terms

- **Smoothed income**: the year's income brought back to an average month, bonuses included.
- **Net and gross**: gross is the salary before social contributions, net what is paid before the income tax withheld at source.
- **Withholding at source**: French income tax taken from the salary each month, at a rate the administration sets and you can adjust.
