Everything the household owns that is neither an investment nor a property: a car, gold coins, a painting, a collection. Nid keeps their value over time and counts it in net worth.

## The questions it answers

- What is our car worth today, compared with what we paid?
- How much do our valuables weigh in our net worth?
- Which assets are shared between us, which are private?

## What Nid does

- **Five kinds of asset**: vehicle, precious metal, work of art, collectible, other.
- **A value is always there**: the purchase price with its date, or a current value; estimates are added over time.
- **For a precious metal**, the weight in grams and the fineness in ‰.
- **Shared or private**, with a share per owner, like the rest of the household.
- **In net worth**: each asset counts at its latest value, under « Other assets » on the dashboard.

## The terms

- **Fineness**: the share of pure metal, in thousandths. Pure gold is 999.9 ‰.
- **Value**: the latest known estimate; with no estimate, the purchase price stands in.
