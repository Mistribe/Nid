Everything the household owns that is neither an investment nor a property: a car, gold coins, a painting, a collection. Nid keeps their value over time and counts it in net worth.

## The questions it answers

- What is our car worth today, compared with what we paid?
- How much do our valuables weigh in our net worth?
- Which assets are shared between us, which are private?

## What Nid does

- **Five kinds of asset**: vehicle, precious metal, work of art, collectible, other.
- **A value is always there**: the purchase price with its date, or a current value; estimates are added over time.
- **For a precious metal**, the weight in grams and the fineness in ‰.
- **For a vehicle**, the registration date, the mileage with the date of its reading (updated in a click), the fiscal horsepower and the energy.
- **Owned or leased**: a lease (LOA, LLD) is a contract, not an asset. Nid does not count it in net worth and offers to track it as a subscription.
- **Linked subscriptions**: a car insurance, a maintenance contract… is linked to the asset from the subscription or from the asset's page, which lists its subscriptions with their price and next due date. Deleting the asset keeps the subscription.
- **A vehicle's running costs**: servicing, repairs, tyres… go in the « Costs » tab of its page, with the year's total worked out by Nid and the invoice attached. A payment from your bank is linked to the vehicle from « Transactions »: the cost is proposed for you, with the category you chose, and never typed twice.
- **Shared or private**, with a share per owner, like the rest of the household.
- **In net worth**: each asset counts at its latest value, under « Other assets » on the dashboard.

## The terms

- **Fineness**: the share of pure metal, in thousandths. Pure gold is 999.9 ‰.
- **Fiscal horsepower**: the number of CV written on the registration certificate.
- **Value**: the latest known estimate; with no estimate, the purchase price stands in.
