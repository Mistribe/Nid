Everything the household owns that is neither an investment nor a property: a car, gold coins, a painting, a collection. Nid keeps their value over time and counts it in net worth.

## The questions it answers

- What is our car worth today, compared with what we paid?
- How much do our valuables weigh in our net worth?
- Which assets are shared between us, which are private?

## What Nid does

- **Five kinds of asset**: vehicle, precious metal, work of art, collectible, other.
- **A value is always there**: the purchase price with its date, or a current value; estimates are added over time. When an old purchase price is all there is (over a year for a vehicle or a precious metal, over three years for the rest), Nid counts it among the values to update and the asset's page asks for a recent value.
- **For a precious metal**, the weight in grams and the fineness in ‰.
- **For a vehicle**, the registration date, the mileage with the date of its reading (updated in a click), the fiscal horsepower, the energy and, if you wish, the yearly loss of value: the displayed value is then an estimate, worked out from the latest known value.
- **Owned or leased**: a lease (LOA, LLD) is a contract, not an asset. Nid does not count it in net worth and offers to track it as a subscription.
- **Linked subscriptions**: a car insurance, a maintenance contract… is linked to the asset from the subscription or from the asset's page, which lists its subscriptions with their price and next due date. Deleting the asset keeps the subscription.
- **A linked loan**: a loan is linked to an asset from the loan's page (« Financed item ») or from the « Loan » tab of the asset's page, which shows the capital still owed and the net value: the asset's value less that capital. Net worth already counts the loan once: the net value is only a display. A financed asset cannot be deleted while the loan is linked.
- **A vehicle's running costs**: servicing, repairs, tyres, fuel or charging… go in the « Costs » tab of its page, with the year's total worked out by Nid and the invoice attached. A payment from your bank is linked to the vehicle from « Transactions »: the cost is recorded for you, with the category you chose (« Carburant / recharge », fuel or charging, for a fill-up): it is never typed twice.
- **What a vehicle really costs**: at the top of the « Costs » tab, Nid works out its cost per month over the last 12 months or over a year: its running costs, fuel, insurance and subscriptions, the cost of its loan and the value it loses. Each part links to its source; a part Nid does not know is marked « Non renseigné » (not filled in) rather than counted as zero. With two mileage readings, it also gives the cost per kilometre.
- **Shared or private**, with a share per owner, like the rest of the household. The list gathers the assets of the same person, or the same couple, on one row with their total, to unfold; a switch puts each back on its own row.
- **In net worth**: each asset counts at its latest value, under « Other assets » on the dashboard.

## The terms

- **Fineness**: the share of pure metal, in thousandths. Pure gold is 999.9 ‰.
- **Fiscal horsepower**: the number of CV written on the registration certificate.
- **Yearly loss of value**: a percentage applied each year to the starting value; a new value you enter restarts the calculation.
- **Value**: the latest known estimate; with no estimate, the purchase price stands in.
- **Cost of ownership**: what the vehicle costs over a period, brought back to a month. A loan counts only its interest, insurance and fees: the capital repaid pays for the car, whose loss of value is already counted.
