Nid estimates the household's French income tax from what it already knows: income, rents, investments, children, childcare costs. It compares the estimate with your tax notices and lets you simulate a change before you make it.

## The questions it answers

- How much income tax will we pay this year?
- Is our withholding too high or too low?
- How much would a PER contribution save?
- Which tax bracket are we in?

## What Nid does

- **The year's estimate**, on the current scale, with the computation in detail.
- **Withholding at source** compared with the real rate, and what to adjust.
- **The tax notices** of each year, compared with the estimate.
- **Simulations**: a PER contribution, the real-expenses regime for rents, one more child.
- **Suggestions** with figures, to pay less, and what each one saves.
- **The household's tax profile** and help with the return.

## The terms

- **Parts**: the household's number of shares in the French family quotient: 2 for a married or PACS couple, plus half a share for each of the first two children.
- **Marginal bracket**: the rate applied to the last euro earned; it does not apply to the whole income.
- **Average rate**: the tax divided by taxable income: what the tax really represents.
- **Micro-foncier and réel**: two ways to declare rents in France: a flat 30 % allowance, or the real charges deducted.
