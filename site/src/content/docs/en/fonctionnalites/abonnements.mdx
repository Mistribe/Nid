Subscriptions rarely cost what their price says: a free month, a launch price, a year paid up front. Nid tracks them the way they are sold, and works out the real cost, per month and per year.

## The questions it answers

- What do our subscriptions really cost, per month and per year?
- When does the free trial end, and what is the price after?
- Until when can we cancel?
- What will be charged in the next twelve months?

## What Nid does

- **Each kind of offer has its own guided form**: recurring, prepaid with free months, a bundle with a purchase, a gift card, a trial or launch price, then what happens next.
- **The real cost and the amortized cost**: a purchase that covers several months is spread over them.
- **Any currency**, converted at the European Central Bank's rates.
- **A provider catalog** that fills in the name and the icon.
- **The contract**: anniversary date, notice period, commitment end, and the last day to cancel.
- **Insurance**: what is covered, the guarantees, the contract number and the insurer's contact.
- **Bank debits** are matched to the subscription, and an unknown recurring debit becomes a subscription proposal.

## The terms

- **Amortized cost**: a purchase's price spread over the months it covers: a year paid up front counts for one twelfth each month.
- **Notice period**: the time to give before the anniversary date to cancel; Nid works out the last day to do it.
- **ECB rate**: the reference exchange rate the European Central Bank publishes every day.
