Nid reads your accounts' balances and transactions, read-only. It matches them to what it expects (subscriptions, loan payments, rents, transfers) and proposes the links: a few minutes a month keep it current.

## The questions it answers

- Where did our money go this month?
- Does this debit match the expected subscription?
- How much is left before the end of the month?

## What Nid does

- **A bank connection** through Enable Banking, read-only: no payment is possible.
- **Or statement files** in OFX or CAMT.053, downloaded from the bank.
- **Matching**: each transaction is linked to the charge it pays, as a proposal to approve.
- **Recurring debits** that are spotted become a subscription proposal.
- **Spending by category**, with budgets and what is left this month.
- **Each adult** links their own accounts; a joint account is seen by both.

## The terms

- **Enable Banking**: a licensed provider that gives read access to bank accounts, with your consent, bank by bank.
- **OFX, CAMT.053**: two statement formats most banks offer for download.
- **Matching**: linking a bank transaction to the expected expense it settles.
