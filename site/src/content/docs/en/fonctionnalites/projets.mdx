Nid helps the household look ahead: a savings goal to reach, net worth in ten or twenty years, retirement, or a property purchase you are considering.

## The questions it answers

- How much should we put aside each month for our trip?
- What will our net worth look like in 20 years?
- Can we buy this property, and what would it change?
- What does our retirement change?

<Screen name="projets" alt="The demo household's savings goals: holders, progress, amount reached and target, deadline and monthly amount needed." />

## What Nid does

- **Savings goals** with a target, a deadline, their progress and the monthly amount needed.
- **The net worth projection** over 5, 10, 20 or 30 years, with a « 9 cases out of 10 » range.
- **Retirement** for each adult, in today's euros, and the financial independence curve.
- **« What if » scenarios**: a property purchase in four steps (property, financing, loans, assumptions), a sale, scenarios compared side by side.

## The terms

- **Today's euros**: future amounts corrected for inflation, to compare them with what you know.
- **Range**: the gap between a bad case and a good case: 9 times out of 10, the result falls inside.
